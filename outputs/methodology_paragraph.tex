Literature was retrieved in October 2026 through the OpenAlex and arXiv programming interfaces using the search strings in Table~\ref{tab:search}, restricted to 2015--2026 for detection-oriented studies, and was complemented by backward and forward citation tracing; foundational physics and protocol papers of any date were retained. The searches returned 5,060 records, and 3,764 remained after de-duplication by DOI and title. Keyword rules applied to titles and abstracts retained 894 candidate records: 414 on detection in quantum communication systems, 325 on quantum machine learning for intrusion or anomaly detection, and 155 on attacks and vulnerabilities of quantum communication. Candidates were prioritized by relevance, citation impact, and recency rather than read exhaustively. A check against 31 references already known to be relevant showed that the searches retrieved 24 of them (77\%), so recall is incomplete, and the other 7 were added manually. The resulting corpus contains 126 references. Of these, 74 are cited in the threat, detection, evaluation, and challenge sections (Sections~\ref{sec:threat_landscape}--\ref{sec:challenges_future}) and 52 are cited only in the introduction, this section, and the background. By publication year, 51 references date from 2023--2026, 19 from 2020--2022, and 56 from 2019 or earlier, of which 29 are cited only in the introduction and background.
